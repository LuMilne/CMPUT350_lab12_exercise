Q3. What happens to the bounding boxes with update + fullRefit?
    The bounding boxes continue to redraw around the groups that were initially formed. 
    The tree does not restructure itself when objects drift apart.

Q4. What happens with reset (rebuild every frame) instead?
    The tree is cleared of existing nodes and rebuilt based on the new conditions.
    Circles that have drifted apart now group with closer circles.

    I do not see circles with their sides cut off, but I anticipated that it would be due to either:
        1. Circles drawn at edge of the window do not complete their shape, as their restricted position 
        in the window does not factor their radius extending beyond the border, or
        2. Circles that span a bounding box for a separate branch may be cutoff by the box
    I will have to assume that option 1 it the intended meaning.
